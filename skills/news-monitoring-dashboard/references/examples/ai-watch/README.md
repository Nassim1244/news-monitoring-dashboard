# Example: AI watch

A real setup, built with this method in November 2026 and running every week since. Anonymized: no names, no page links, no personal paths. People tags are replaced by placeholders.

| File | What to look at |
|---|---|
| `watch-settings.md` | Settings after the interview |
| `MyTaste.md` | Taste profile after a full grilling session: ~60 tags, precise Signal / Watch / Briefs / Noise / To try rules, 15 rated seed examples |
| `sources.md` | 24 sources, all tested, with roles (item, crosscheck, totry) and the excluded list with reasons |

Use it to see the expected depth. Do not copy it as is: your own rules and examples are what make the sorting work for you.
