## Bucket hints
- Signal by default: major policy or funding program taking effect; large plant opening or closure; lasting price or supply shift.
- Watch: pilots, first deployments, announced but not built projects.
- To try: free tools, methods with measured results, public funding calls open now.
- Briefs: big acquisition, major accident, CEO change at a major player.
- Noise: trade show announcements without customers, vendor case studies without numbers, award news.
