## Bucket hints
- Signal by default: major algorithm or ranking change on a main platform; privacy rule taking effect; lasting shift in how people search or buy.
- Watch: beta features on main platforms; first data on a new channel.
- To try: free tools, templates, new features usable now, proven techniques with data.
- Briefs: big agency merger, platform outage, landmark fine.
- Noise: listicles ("10 tips"), award news, opinion pieces without data, minor UI changes.
